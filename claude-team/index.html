The two-week rollout

Week 0 · Seed

Make the setup real

  • Add CLAUDE.md + a starter .claude/ to your top 2–3 repos
  • Wire one MCP server everyone needs (GitHub / Linear)
  • Pick 3 champions who already "get it"
Week 1 · Show

Convert the skeptics

  • 45-min live demo: drive a real ticket end to end
  • Open a #claude-wins channel
  • Ship the "5 things to try this week" note
Week 2 · Stick

Make it a habit

  • 30-min weekly office hours (champions unblock everyone)
  • Everyone contributes one skill / CLAUDE.md improvement
  • Run a with-skills vs without A/B; share the delta

Access ≠ adoption — manufacture the awareness

  • Live demo, real code. Nothing converts a skeptic like watching it fix an actual bug and open the PR. Not slides — a terminal.
  • A #claude-wins channel. Social proof compounds. One good win spawns five imitators; keep them visible.
  • The skills repo as a team asset. Everyone contributes improvements — adoption is a culture, not a license count.
  • Weekly office hours. The 3 who get it unblock the 10 who don't. Adoption is a mentoring problem.
  • A friendly internal experiment. Data beats hype — run with-skills vs without on a real feature and post the result.
  • Five starter workflows, documented. Kill the blank-page problem — tell people exactly what to try first.
